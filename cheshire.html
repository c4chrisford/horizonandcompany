The Cheshire Aesthetic

Atmospheric Settings

Historic City Architecture

For couples drawn to the rich heritage of locations like Chester, the medieval architecture and historic streetscapes offer an incredibly romantic, timeless backdrop.

Our unobtrusive videography thrives in these environments. We use the stunning natural light and historic textures of the city to craft a film that feels deeply atmospheric, highlighting the juxtaposition of modern celebrations against ancient stone.

Beautiful Country Estates

The epitome of the classic Cheshire wedding. Flawlessly curated spaces - from grand architecture to elegant walled gardens - provide a seamless journey for your day.

Our storytelling focuses on this elegant flow. We capture the natural beauty of the estate grounds alongside the intimate human moments, creating a highly polished, editorial film that reflects the true character of your day.

Rustic Barns & Woodland Retreats

Relaxed, rustic, and breathtakingly natural. Venues wrapped in deep parkland or beautiful converted barns offer an intimate, warm atmosphere that translates beautifully to film.

We focus on rich colour grading and capturing the sun-dappled textures of the landscape, whilst quietly documenting your celebration as it naturally unfolds.

Frequently Asked Questions

To keep things simple and fair, dates are secured on a first-come, first-served basis. Once you confirm you'd like to book, we require a 25% non-refundable booking fee and a signed digital contract to lock your date in our diary. The remaining balance isn't due until 30 days before your wedding.

Travel within 50 miles of our base is completely free of charge. For weddings further afield across the UK or Europe, we simply charge standard travel and accommodation costs at cost-value, which we will agree with you upfront before you book.

Music is 50% of the emotion in any film. With a background as a signed music producer and director - running a recording studio in Liverpool, shooting music videos, and having tracks played on national radio - audio is my secret weapon. While copyright laws mean we cannot use standard commercial pop songs, I use my industry experience to source and edit premium, cinematic soundtracks. I do not just drop a nice song over your footage; I score your film like a movie, perfectly matching the pacing, energy, and unique atmosphere of your day.

We believe in taking the time to edit your film perfectly rather than rushing it. During peak wedding season, our standard delivery time is 6-10 weeks. Your films will be delivered in stunning 4K via a private online gallery that you can download from and share with family and friends.
