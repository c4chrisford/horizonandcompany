Behind The Lens

Calm on the day.
Particular in the edit.

Horizon & Company was built around a love of storytelling, atmosphere and the small moments that give a wedding its personality.

My approach on the day is deliberately unobtrusive. I want you, your family and your friends to forget about the camera rather than spend the celebration performing for it.

A background in music production brings particular attention to sound, pacing and atmosphere in the edit, helping each film feel immersive rather than simply a collection of beautiful shots.

The aim is simple: something beautifully crafted that still feels unmistakably like your wedding.
