Cinematic Wedding Films

Films That Feel Like Your Day

At Horizon & Company we believe the best wedding films feel natural, emotional and timeless.

Our approach is relaxed and unobtrusive, allowing real moments to unfold while we capture them with a cinematic eye.

Based in the North West and filming across the UK and Europe, we focus on storytelling rather than staged moments.

The result is a wedding film that reflects the atmosphere of your day and the people who matter most.

Every film is crafted with careful editing, natural colour grading and professional audio so your memories feel as vivid years from now as they did in the moment.

Some couples want a relaxed highlights film that captures the feeling of the day. Others prefer a deeper narrative edit featuring speeches and ceremony moments.

We tailor each film to match the tone and personality of your wedding.
