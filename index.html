Behind The Lens

Quiet on the day.
Thoughtful in the edit.

On the wedding day, the approach is deliberately relaxed and unobtrusive. The less you think about the camera, the more honest the moments become.

In the edit, the speeches, reactions and relationships begin to shape the film. Some moments need space, others need pace, and sometimes a few words matter more than the most beautiful shot.

A background in music production brings particular attention to sound, rhythm and atmosphere, helping the finished film feel immersive rather than simply a collection of highlights.
