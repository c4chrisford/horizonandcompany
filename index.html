The Approach

Natural & Authentic Storytelling

A wedding is more than a schedule of events; it is a unique energy that exists only once.

Our films are designed to preserve that atmosphere. From the grand, joyful celebrations to the quiet, nervous exchanges, we focus on the authentic character of your day, ensuring the resulting film feels as real as the memories themselves.

We believe you should be completely present on your wedding day, which is why we prioritise documentary storytelling over awkward, staged poses.

You won't find us orchestrating moments or making you repeat actions for the camera. By blending quietly into the background, we capture the laughter, the tears, and the subtle interactions that make your story completely personal.

The result is a beautifully crafted, timeless legacy that lets you relive the genuine emotion of your celebration for years to come.

The Intention

Behind the Lens

Horizon & Co was built on a love of storytelling, atmosphere and the moments that give a wedding day its meaning.

The work is shaped by years of experience across wedding films, music videos and high-end creative productions, bringing a cinematic eye together with a calm, unobtrusive presence on the day.

A background in music production and studio work brings particular care to sound, pacing and atmosphere, allowing each film to feel immersive, natural and emotionally rich.

The result is a wedding film that feels effortless, honest and beautifully crafted, preserving the emotion of the day exactly as it was lived.

To keep things simple and fair, dates are secured on a first-come, first-served basis. Once you confirm you'd like to book, we require a 25% non-refundable booking fee and a signed digital contract to lock your date in our diary. The remaining balance isn't due until 30 days before your wedding.

Travel within 50 miles of our base is completely free of charge. For weddings further afield across the UK or Europe, we simply charge standard travel and accommodation costs at cost value, which we will agree with you upfront before you book.

Music plays a huge role in the emotion and atmosphere of a wedding film. Because of copyright restrictions, we are not able to use standard commercial songs in your final film, but this is also what allows the soundtrack to feel more cinematic and carefully considered.

With a background in music production and audio-led storytelling, each film is crafted with just as much attention to what is heard as what is seen. Rather than simply placing a track over the footage, we carefully shape the pacing, tone and soundtrack so the finished film feels natural, immersive and true to the emotion of your day.

The result is a wedding film that feels personal, elevated and beautifully matched to the moments it captures.
