More Than The Highlights

You spend months planning it. Then somehow, it's over in a day.

Photographs preserve how everything looked. Film brings back how it moved, sounded and felt — including the parts you didn't get to see yourself.

01 Before It Begins

The anticipation.

The nerves, the last-minute adjustments, the people arriving and the quiet moments before everything suddenly becomes real.

02 Hear It Again

The words.

Your vows. The crack in someone's voice. The laughter during the ceremony. The things people said that become more valuable with every year that passes.

03 See What You Missed

The reactions.

Your parents watching you. Your friends laughing behind you. Small moments happening elsewhere while your attention is understandably somewhere else.

04 Remember The People

The personalities.

The stories in the speeches, the terrible jokes, the hugs, the voices and mannerisms that make your wedding unmistakably yours.

05 Feel The Atmosphere

The energy.

Movement, music, crowded dance floors, people pulling each other into frame and the point in the evening when everyone finally stops caring about the camera.

Behind The Camera

Natural enough to feel like your wedding. Considered enough to feel like a film.

I'm not there to turn your wedding into a production. Most of the day is captured as it naturally happens, with just enough direction when it genuinely helps.

That means less performing for the camera, fewer forced moments and more space for the day to actually unfold.

The cinematic part comes afterwards — through the way those moments are selected, paced, coloured, scored and connected.
