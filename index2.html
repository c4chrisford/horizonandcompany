Behind The Lens

Quiet while it's happening. Particular about what happens afterwards.

You shouldn't spend your wedding thinking about the person filming it.

On the day, I keep things deliberately relaxed and unobtrusive. There may occasionally be a little guidance where it helps, but genuine moments are never repeatedly staged for the camera.

The detailed work happens later. Speeches, reactions, music, natural audio and imagery are brought together to find the rhythm and emotional shape of the film.

My background in music production has a big influence here. Sound isn't something added underneath the pictures at the end; it's part of how the story is built.
