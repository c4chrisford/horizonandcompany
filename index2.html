What Film Gives You

Don't just remember the day. Step back inside it.

Photographs preserve moments beautifully. Film brings back the movement, the voices, the laughter and all the little things happening in between.

YOUR STORY
01 Their Voices

Hear it the way it really sounded.

Your vows. A parent's speech. The joke that nearly doesn't land. A voice cracking halfway through a sentence. The laughter immediately afterwards.

Some memories are as much about how something was said as the words themselves.
02 The Atmosphere

Remember what the room felt like.

The quiet before everything begins. Everybody cheering at once. A room full of laughter. The little glance between you when it suddenly feels real.

Memory tends to keep the main events. Film can bring back everything happening around them.
03 Your People

Your favourite people, completely themselves.

Conversations across the room. Friends catching up. Your parents laughing together. Someone reacting to a speech without realising there's a camera anywhere near them.

Often it's the completely unscripted moments that feel most personal afterwards.
04 Everything Around You

See the parts of the day you weren't watching.

You can only be in one conversation at once. Somewhere else, somebody is laughing. Someone is hugging an old friend. Your family are reacting to something you're too busy living to notice.

A film gives you another perspective on the same day — including moments you didn't know had happened.
05 The Celebration

And then everyone forgets about the schedule.

The first dance. The drinks. The terrible dancing. The friend who insisted they wouldn't get up. The hugs, singing and complete lack of coordination once the evening takes over.

Not every memorable part of a wedding has to be serious.

Behind The Lens

Quiet while it's happening. Particular about what happens afterwards.

You shouldn't spend your wedding thinking about the person filming it.

On the day, I keep things relaxed and unobtrusive. There may occasionally be a little guidance where it helps, but the real moments are allowed to happen naturally.

Afterwards, I spend time finding the story already inside the footage. Speeches, reactions, natural audio, music and imagery are brought together around the personality and rhythm of the day.

My creative background stretches beyond wedding filmmaking. Years spent working with music, moving image and different kinds of productions have shaped the way I think about story, timing, sound and the person ultimately watching.

The finished film isn't built around a collection of favourite shots. It's built around the moments that make the wedding recognisably yours.

Story first. Beautiful images in service of it. Natural enough to feel like your wedding. Considered enough to feel like a film.
