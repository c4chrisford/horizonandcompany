More Than The Highlights

The day happens once. Your film lets you notice more of it.

Not because every second needs recording, but because movement, sound and the people around you tell parts of the story that photographs alone cannot.

01 Their Voices

Not only what they said. How they said it.

Vows, speeches, laughter, pauses and the moments where somebody's voice gives away exactly how much it means.

02 Before It Begins

The part of the day where everything is still ahead of you.

Hair half finished. People drifting in and out. Nerves, jokes, bags everywhere and moments that feel ordinary until the day is over.

03 Your People

Some of your favourite moments may happen without you.

Your parents together. Friends catching up. Someone laughing across the room. Reactions you never knew happened until you watched them back.

04 When Everyone Lets Go

The formalities end. The personalities come out.

The drinks, the terrible dancing, the people singing too loudly and that point in the evening where nobody is thinking about the camera anymore.

Behind The Lens

Your wedding first. The film comes from it.

You shouldn't spend your wedding feeling as though you've been placed onto a film set.

I work quietly, stay out of the way where possible and let the real interactions happen. If a little direction would genuinely help, I'll give it — but I'm not interested in endlessly recreating moments for the camera.

The more intensive work happens afterwards. Vows, speeches, reactions, natural sound, music and imagery are shaped around the rhythm and personality of your particular wedding.

01 Relaxed on the day

Enough guidance to help. Enough space for you to forget I'm there.

02 Real audio

Vows, speeches and natural sound become part of the storytelling.

03 Edited around you

Pace and music follow your wedding, rather than forcing every couple into one template.

Natural enough to feel like your wedding. Considered enough to feel like a film.
