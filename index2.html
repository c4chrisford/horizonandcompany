Behind The Lens

Quiet while it's happening. Obsessive about what happens afterwards.

You shouldn't spend your wedding thinking about the person filming it.

On the day, I keep things deliberately relaxed and unobtrusive. There may occasionally be a little guidance where it helps, but genuine moments are never repeatedly staged for the camera.

The detailed work happens later. Speeches, reactions, natural audio, music and imagery are brought together to find the rhythm and emotional shape of the film.

My creative background stretches beyond wedding filmmaking. Years spent working with music, moving image and different kinds of productions have shaped the way I think about story, timing, sound and the person ultimately watching.

So the edit isn't built around a collection of favourite shots. It's built around what makes the day feel like yours.

Story first. Beautiful images in service of it. That is the difference between a wedding montage and a wedding film.
