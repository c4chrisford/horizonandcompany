The Aesthetic

Cinematic storytelling in a beautiful city.

From the striking scale of the Georgian Quarter to the elegant light of the waterfront, Liverpool provides a dramatic and timeless backdrop for a wedding film, whether you are celebrating in a historic building or a modern city hotel.

We specialise in natural, documentary-led videography. We do not dictate or stage moments. Instead, we blend quietly into the background, capturing the subtle interactions, the nervous laughter, and the joyful celebrations exactly as they happen.

"We focus on the moments you lived, and the ones that you missed. Creating a legacy that feels as authentic as your memories."

Venues We Love

St George's Hall

A venue that truly defines grandeur. St George's Hall requires a videography style that can match its architectural scale while still isolating the intimate human moments.

Our cinematic approach is designed to capture the atmospheric light and historic textures of the space, ensuring the venue enhances your story rather than overpowering it.

Sefton Park Palm House

An oasis of natural light and botanical elegance. The Palm House offers an incredibly unique setting that translates beautifully to film.

We focus on rich, vibrant colour grading to bring out the lush greens and stunning glass architecture, creating a film that feels both organic and highly cinematic.

City Centre Churches & Hotels

Liverpool offers a striking contrast of heritage architecture against sleek, modern lines. Whether you are marrying at Our Lady and St Nicholas or a boutique Hope Street hotel, the atmosphere is unmatched.

We use the rich textures and natural light of your chosen venue to craft a film that feels deeply atmospheric, highlighting the beautiful juxtaposition of timeless tradition right in the heart of the city.

To keep things simple and fair, dates are secured on a first-come, first-served basis. Once you confirm you'd like to book, we require a 25% non-refundable booking fee and a signed digital contract to lock your date in our diary. The remaining balance isn't due until 30 days before your wedding.

Travel within 50 miles of our base is completely free of charge. For weddings further afield across the UK or Europe, we simply charge standard travel and accommodation costs at cost value, which we will agree with you upfront before you book.

Music plays a huge role in the emotion and atmosphere of a wedding film. Because of copyright restrictions, we are not able to use standard commercial songs in your final film, but this is also what allows the soundtrack to feel more cinematic and carefully considered.

With a background in music production and audio-led storytelling, each film is crafted with just as much attention to what is heard as what is seen. Rather than simply placing a track over the footage, we carefully shape the pacing, tone and soundtrack so the finished film feels natural, immersive and true to the emotion of your day.

The result is a wedding film that feels personal, elevated and beautifully matched to the moments it captures.
