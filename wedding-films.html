The Approach

Your wedding.
Not a production.

The aim is not to spend your wedding day creating footage for a film. The aim is to let you experience the wedding while the film is created around what genuinely happens.

We work quietly and unobtrusively, then bring the cinematic side in afterwards through composition, professional audio, colour, pacing and storytelling.

The result should feel polished enough to watch like a film, while still feeling unmistakably like your own wedding.
