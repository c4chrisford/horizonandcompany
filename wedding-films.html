The Approach

A film that feels like you.

We don't arrive with a template for what your wedding film should look or sound like. We pay attention to the people, conversations, relationships and atmosphere around us.

On the day, the approach is relaxed and unobtrusive so genuine moments can happen without constantly being directed for the camera.

In the edit, those moments determine the creative choices. Sound, music, dialogue, colour and pacing are used to reflect the character of the wedding rather than make every celebration feel the same.
