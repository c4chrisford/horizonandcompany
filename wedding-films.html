01 Cheshire Wedding Film

Andy & Lesley

Grosvenor Pulford Hotel & Spa · Cheshire

Emotional. Reflective. Full of history.

This film needed room to breathe. The speeches carried years of friendship, family history and the sense that two people had found each other at exactly the right point in their lives.

Why This Film Feels Different

The speeches became the emotional spine. The edit gives the words time to land, then lets the reactions around them do part of the storytelling. A slower soundtrack, longer moments and quieter transitions keep the film reflective rather than forcing energy that was never really there.

Kirsty & Connor

Thornton Hall Hotel & Spa · Wirral

Fun. Carefree. Full of energy.

The Approach

Quiet on the day. Particular in the edit.

You should not spend your wedding performing for the camera.

I work around the day as it naturally happens, stepping in with a little guidance only when it genuinely helps. Most of the time, I am looking for the moments that happen because nobody has been asked to create them.

Afterwards, the work becomes much more deliberate. I go through the footage, speeches and natural audio looking for the emotional thread that already exists, then build the film around it.
