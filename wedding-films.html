The Approach

Your wedding.
Not a production.

You shouldn't spend your wedding day thinking about the camera. Our documentary-led approach means we work quietly around the celebration rather than constantly directing it.

The cinematic side happens through composition, sound, colour and editing — not by turning genuine moments into staged ones.
