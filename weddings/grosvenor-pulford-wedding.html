Grosvenor Pulford Wedding Film

Natural moments in a beautiful Cheshire setting.

The aim was simple: preserve the atmosphere of the day without turning the wedding into a film set.

Andy and Lesley's wedding at Grosvenor Pulford Hotel & Spa was filmed with a documentary-led approach, allowing the celebration to unfold naturally while quietly capturing the reactions, conversations and moments happening around them.

Rather than repeatedly staging scenes for the camera, the focus remained on genuine interactions and the character of the day itself.

Grosvenor Pulford offers a mixture of elegant interiors, landscaped grounds and intimate spaces, giving a wedding film plenty of visual variety while still keeping the couple and their guests at the heart of the story.

The finished film combines cinematic composition, natural colour, professional audio and carefully considered pacing to create something that feels polished without losing the spontaneity of the celebration.

A wedding film should not simply show what happened. It should bring back how the day felt.

“We focus on the moments you lived, and the ones that you missed. Creating a legacy that feels as authentic as your memories.”
