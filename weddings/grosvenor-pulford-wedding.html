The Wedding

Emotional.

Andy and Lesley's wedding at Grosvenor Pulford Hotel & Spa had a very different energy to a fast-paced celebration. The emotion came from the people around them, the years behind them and the sense that this day represented the beginning of another chapter.

The speeches naturally looked backwards as well as forwards. Friends spoke about the difference Lesley had made to Andy's life, while Andy himself spoke about feeling lucky to have found the person he wanted to spend the rest of it with.

That gave the film its direction. Rather than chasing constant energy, the edit was allowed to breathe - giving the words, reactions and quieter moments the space they needed.
