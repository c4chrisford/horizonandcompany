Kirsty and Connor's wedding at Thornton Hall Hotel & Spa was the kind of celebration that never felt too serious. From the ceremony through to the speeches and celebrations, their personalities came through in every part of the day.

Rather than trying to manufacture those moments, the focus was simply on being there when they happened - the glances, reactions, laughter and little exchanges between the couple, their families and friends.

The resulting film reflects exactly that: relaxed, emotional and full of the humour and energy that made their wedding completely their own.
